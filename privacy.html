Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Crownfall is a single-player game. We designed it to work without collecting personal data. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Data we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share personal data.

Data stored on your device

Saved games and settings are stored locally on your device, or in your browser's storage for the web version. This data never leaves your device and we cannot access it. Deleting the app or clearing your browser's site data removes it.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security and operation of its service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Support requests

If you contact us through our public issue tracker, the information you choose to post is public and handled under GitHub's terms. Do not include personal information.

Children

Because the game collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

Changes

If we ever add a feature that processes personal data, such as cloud saves or crash reporting, we will update this policy before that feature ships and change the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on our issue tracker.
