Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Crownfall can be played on your own or in World, its shared online mode. Playing on your own sends nothing to us. World needs a small anonymous account, and this page explains what that means.

Who runs this

Crownfall is run by Raztech Innovations. Questions about this policy or your data: raztechsupport@icloud.com.

Playing on your own

We collect nothing. There are no ads and no tracking. Saved games and settings stay on your device. If you turn on play stats, that log also stays on your device. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Buying the Full Game

The Full Game is bought through Apple. We never see your payment details, and the game checks the purchase on your device with Apple, so nothing about it reaches our server.

iCloud and Game Center

On an iPhone with iCloud, your saved match and Quick Battle level also go to your own private iCloud storage, so they follow you to your other devices. We can't see them. Deleting your save in the game deletes it there too.

The game signs you in to Game Center when it opens. Apple runs Game Center, and we receive nothing from it.

What World keeps

  • a generated player name
  • a scrambled copy of your device's access key
  • your gold, plots and village saves
  • your battle records
  • when the server last saw you
  • which alliance you are in
  • if you sign in with Apple or Google, a scrambled copy of the ID they give this game
  • if you sign in with Apple, a key Apple gives us so that deleting your World account also ends its link to your Apple ID

We never ask for your real name, email or a device ID. Signing in with Apple or Google is your choice, and it is what lets your World account come with you to a new phone. We never ask them for your email, and they know only that you signed in to Crownfall. The server uses your internet address to reply to you and to limit abuse. It isn't saved with your account.

What other players can see

  • your generated name
  • your plots and whether you are at war
  • a read-only copy of your villages
  • the name of your alliance
  • battle reports, to the players in that battle

Alliance members also see each other's names and the location of each member's first village. No one can see when you were last online. World has no chat and no free text.

Why we keep it

To run the game you asked to play.

Where it is kept

On a server in Germany, hosted by dogado GmbH.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. A plot bought with gold goes back to the world, with its village, if you haven't opened World for 180 days. An account no one has used for two years is deleted, unless it holds a plot bought with money. Deleted data leaves our backups within 30 days.

Your choices

On the World map, Settings lets you export everything we hold on you or delete it for good. Deleting it also ends a Sign in with Apple link, so Crownfall leaves the list of apps in your Apple ID settings. Leaving an alliance takes one tap. If you haven't signed in with Apple or Google, we can't tell which account is yours once you lose your device or delete the game, so delete your World data first. When you delete it, battles other players took part in stay in their reports, with your name removed.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security and operation of its service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Support requests

If you contact us through our public issue tracker, the information you choose to post is public and handled under GitHub's terms. Do not include personal information there: email us instead.

Children

We don't ask anyone's age. Names are generated and there is no chat, so players can't share personal details in the game.

Changes

We update this page and its date before any change to what we collect.
