Support

Crownfall is in development. We read every report and use it to make the game better.

Report a problem or ask a question

Open an issue on our public issue tracker. Please include your device, its iOS or macOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. Do not post personal information.

Rather not post in public? Email raztechsupport@icloud.com. Crownfall is made by Raztech Innovations.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Crownfall is a single-player game and does not require an account.

Where are my saved games? On your device, and in your own iCloud if it is on. World villages are also kept on the World server.

Are there in-app purchases or ads? There are no ads. Crownfall is free to start, and one purchase, the Full Game, unlocks the rest of the campaign, Hard, every mode and the second realm, for good. Nothing in World is for sale.

I bought the Full Game on another device. Open something it unlocks, such as Hard, and tap Restore Purchases. It comes with the Apple ID that bought it, and with Family Sharing.
