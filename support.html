Support

Crownfall is in development. We read every report and use it to make the game better.

Report a problem or ask a question

Open an issue on our public issue tracker. Please include your device, operating system or browser version, and the steps that led to the problem. Do not post personal information.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Crownfall is a single-player game and does not require an account.

Where are my saved games? On your device. In the web version, saves live in your browser's storage. Clearing site data or using private browsing removes them.

Are there in-app purchases or ads? No. Crownfall is a premium game with no ads and no in-app purchases.
