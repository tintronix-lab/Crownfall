The story

  1. The crown of Eldoria has fallen.

    The red host is on the march.

  4. Beyond them, the Bandit Lord waits in his fortress.

    Someone has to ride out and face him.

Questions

What does it cost?

Crownfall is free to start on the App Store, for iPhone and iPad: the first chapter of the campaign, Quick Battle, Today, Conquest and Prosperity, and all of World. One purchase, the Full Game, unlocks the rest of the campaign, Hard, Discovery, Adventure, Landmarks and the second realm, for good. No ads, and nothing in World is for sale.

I've never played a strategy game. Is it for me?

Yes. The goals panel leads you through your first match, Easy gives you room to learn, and in Prosperity your rival never attacks.

Do I need an account or the internet?

No. You play against the computer, and your saves stay on your device. The online mode, World, needs the internet and a small anonymous account. See our privacy policy.

Where does the art come from?

The world and its people are built from free CC0 art by Quaternius, Kenney and KayKit, adapted for the game. Every picture on this page comes from the game itself.
