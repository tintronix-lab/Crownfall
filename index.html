A fantasy strategy game for iPhone. Coming soon to the App Store.

Begin the story

The crown of Eldoria has fallen.

Nobody rules the land now. Every realm that once knelt to the crown has gone its own way, and none of them means to share.

Its realms now fight over the Aether Wells.

Hold a well and its Aether buys arms and wards that no gold can. Lose it, and your rival is the one who grows strong.

Fires burn in the east.

The red host is on the march, and it leaves nothing standing behind it.

Beyond them, the Bandit Lord waits in his fortress.

He took a crown of his own. Someone has to ride out and take it from him.

One town still stands. Yours.

That is where you begin: a handful of villagers, a few spearmen, and an enemy who is already building.

From one Town Centre to a kingdom

Nobody waits for a turn. Across the map, your rival is doing just what you are, and every second you build is a second closer to the fight.

  1. Gather

    Send villagers for food, wood, stone and gold, and win over neutral villages by trade or by keeping them safe.

  2. Build

    Twelve kinds of building, from Houses and Farms to the Stable, the Forge and Watch Towers that shoot arrows.

  3. Research

    Sixteen technologies. Spearmen rise to Pike Wardens, then Royal Guards, with new looks and names.

  4. Fight

    Lead scouts, spearmen, archers, knights and battering rams against the enemy gates.

Three princesses stand with you.

Train them at your Town Centre and turn the tide. Each lends her gift to the units around her, and each has one power of her own, to use yourself or leave on Auto.

  • Jasmin heals.

    Healing Wave mends everyone around her at once.

  • Nadja gives valour.

    Battle Cry makes her soldiers hit harder and march faster.

  • Jing gives speed.

    Gust of Wind speeds up her units and blows enemies back.

Raise the levy. Keep the watch. Burn the red camp.

The story is three missions, and the realm is waiting on all of them.

  • The Standing Stones

    Raise your levy, take the Old Watchstone and hold it.

  • The Long Watch

    Keep your Town Centre standing for eight minutes against three waves.

  • The Red Camp

    Find the war chest, then burn the red camp before its host marches.

From timber fort to crystal crown

Advance through four ages, and every building you own changes the moment you get there.

  1. Age I Frontier

    Timber, thatch and tents.

  2. Age II Settlement

    Stone floors and your colours on the roof tiles.

  3. Age III Kingdom

    Stone keeps, towers and long banners.

  4. Age IV Arcane Crown

    Taller, gilded and glowing with crystals.

The Crownward Compact

Steady and strong.

A medieval kingdom of honey timber and pale stone, with the full set of soldiers.

The Verdant Covenant

Quick and hard to pin down.

Woodland folk who gather wood 20% faster and see further, with Thornguard and Woodland Rangers.

However long you have

Play on our hand-built valley or a new random map, on Easy, Normal or Hard, against a rival in one of four styles.

  • Quick Battle

    Eight minutes, one tap.

    Your base is already running. Win, and the next level waits on a new map with a tougher rival.

  • Conquest

    Destroy the enemy Town Centre.

    The classic match. Play it alone, or two against two with a computer ally at your side.

  • Prosperity

    First town to 1,200 prosperity.

    Your rival never attacks, so this is the one for when you just want to build.

  • Discovery

    First to find 5 treasures.

    Ruins, shrines and caches lie in the fog. Send out your scouts.

  • Adventure

    Bring down the Bandit Lord.

    Lead a Champion who grows stronger with every fight.

  • Landmarks

    Hold every landmark for 3 minutes.

    Take all three and keep them, while your rival comes for them too.

Made for your iPhone

Full real-time strategy in landscape, with no mouse and no tiny menus. Drag to look around, tap to give orders, and let a goals panel walk you through your first match.

Questions

When can I play?

Crownfall is coming soon to the App Store. We'll put the date and price here as soon as we have them.

Which devices will it run on?

iPhone comes first.

I've never played a strategy game. Is it for me?

Yes. The goals panel leads you through your first match, Easy gives you room to learn, and in Prosperity your rival never attacks.

Is there multiplayer?

The main game is played against the computer, alone or beside a computer ally. An online mode, World, is being tried out with friends.

Do I need an account or the internet?

No. The single-player modes need neither, and your saves stay on your device. World needs the internet and a small anonymous account: see our privacy policy.

Where does the art come from?

The world and its people are built from free CC0 art by Quaternius, Kenney and KayKit, adapted for the game. Every picture on this page comes from the game itself.
